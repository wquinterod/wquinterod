ABOUT ME

Getting to know me

My name is Wilson Quintero. I am studying web development and information technology. I enjoy working with computers and finding simple ways to solve problems.

Why I am building this site

Practice makes progress

Every week I learn something new in class. This website gives me a place to try it. I started with a simple page and added more styles, images, and pages over time.

What interests me

I am interested in how a website can help people find information. I am also learning about networking and how different devices communicate.

MY DESIGN JOURNEY

Learning about color and design

When I started this class, choosing colors and creating a good website design was very difficult for me. I struggled a lot with that part. I knew what information I wanted to include, but I was not sure how to make everything look organized and attractive.

During this class, I have learned how to use color combinations, backgrounds, gradients, spacing, CSS Grid, and Flexbox. I am also learning how small design decisions can make a website look more professional and easier to use.

I still have a lot to learn, but I can clearly see how much my work has improved since the first weeks. This portfolio shows my progress, my mistakes, and the new skills I am developing.

My goal is to become a better web developer and continue improving both my coding and design skills.
